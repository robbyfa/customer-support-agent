# Deposit Policy

## Overview

This policy covers how customer deposit issues are handled. Deposits are the primary funding method for customer accounts and must be resolved promptly.

## Processing Times

- Credit/debit card deposits: instant to 5 minutes.
- E-wallet deposits (PayPal, Skrill, Neteller): instant to 10 minutes.
- Bank transfer deposits: 1–3 business days depending on the bank.
- Crypto deposits: 10–30 minutes depending on network confirmation times.

## Missing Deposits

- If a deposit has not been credited within the expected processing time, support agents should:
  1. Verify the transaction details provided by the customer (amount, method, reference).
  2. Check the internal transaction log for any pending or failed entries.
  3. If no record is found, ask the customer for a bank statement or payment confirmation screenshot.
  4. Escalate to the Payments team if the deposit cannot be located within 24 hours.

## Duplicate Charges

- If a customer reports being charged twice for a single deposit:
  1. Verify both charges in the transaction log.
  2. If confirmed as a duplicate, initiate a refund for the extra charge immediately.
  3. The refund should be processed to the original payment method.
  4. Inform the customer of the expected refund timeline.

## Failed Deposits

- Common failure reasons: card declined by issuer, insufficient funds, 3D Secure authentication failure, or payment provider downtime.
- Support agents should advise the customer to try an alternative payment method if the issue persists.
- Do not share specific technical error codes with the customer.

## Deposit Limits

- Minimum deposit: €10.
- Maximum single deposit: €10,000.
- Daily deposit limit: €25,000.
- Customers may request a lower deposit limit for responsible gaming purposes.

## Escalation

- Escalate to the Payments team if: deposit is missing for more than 24 hours, duplicate charges confirmed, or the customer provides proof of payment not reflected in the system.
