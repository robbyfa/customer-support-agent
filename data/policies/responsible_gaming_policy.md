# Responsible Gaming Policy

## Overview

This policy governs how the support team handles responsible gaming matters. Responsible gaming cases are **always classified as sensitive** and require **mandatory human review** before any action is taken.

## Core Principles

- Player safety is the top priority in all responsible gaming interactions.
- Support agents must treat every responsible gaming request with urgency and empathy.
- The customer should never feel judged or discouraged from seeking help.
- All responsible gaming interactions must be logged in the audit trail.

## Self-Exclusion

- Customers may request a self-exclusion period of **24 hours, 7 days, 30 days, 6 months, or permanently**.
- Self-exclusion requests must be processed within **1 hour** of receipt.
- During the self-exclusion period, the customer cannot log in, deposit, place bets, or access promotional offers.
- Self-exclusion cannot be reversed before the selected period expires (except for the 24-hour cooling-off option, which can be lifted after the period ends).
- Permanent self-exclusion is irreversible and the account will be closed.

## Deposit Limits

- Customers may request daily, weekly, or monthly deposit limits at any time.
- Deposit limit decreases take effect **immediately**.
- Deposit limit increases take effect after a **24-hour cooling-off period**.
- Support agents must never encourage a customer to increase their deposit limit.

## Reality Checks

- Reality checks are pop-up reminders displayed during active sessions.
- Customers can set reality check intervals (e.g., every 30 minutes, 60 minutes).
- If a customer requests to disable reality checks, advise them of the feature's purpose but comply with their request.

## Identifying At-Risk Behaviour

- Signs of at-risk behaviour include: chasing losses, erratic deposit patterns, spending more than stated budget, self-reported gambling concerns, requests related to financial hardship.
- If at-risk behaviour is detected during a support interaction, the agent must flag the case for review by the Responsible Gaming team.
- Support agents should never diagnose or label a customer's behaviour. Instead, offer available tools and resources.

## Support Resources

- National gambling helplines should be provided upon request or when at-risk behaviour is identified.
- Available resources: GamCare (UK), Gambling Therapy (international), Spieler-Info (Germany/Austria), Stödlinjen (Sweden).
- Support agents may share helpline links but must not provide counselling or medical advice.

## Escalation

- **All responsible gaming cases must be escalated** to the Responsible Gaming team.
- Self-exclusion requests must be escalated immediately - do not wait for the next business day.
- Cases involving minors or suspected underage gambling must be escalated to Compliance with the highest priority.
