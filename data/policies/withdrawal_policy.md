# Withdrawal Policy

## Overview

This policy governs how customer withdrawal requests are handled by the support team. All agents must follow these guidelines when dealing with withdrawal-related issues.

## Processing Times

- E-wallet withdrawals: processed within 2 hours.
- Credit/debit card withdrawals: 1–3 business days.
- Bank transfer withdrawals: 3–5 business days.
- Processing times start after internal compliance review is completed.

## Failed Withdrawals

- Repeated failed withdrawals must be treated as **high priority**.
- If a customer reports two or more failed withdrawal attempts within 48 hours, the case must be escalated to **Payments Operations** immediately.
- Support agents must **not** tell the customer that the withdrawal is resolved unless payment status has been confirmed by the Payments team.
- Common failure reasons include: payment provider timeout, insufficient funds in the operator account, compliance hold, or incorrect banking details.

## Reversed Withdrawals

- If a withdrawal is reversed, the customer must receive a clear explanation of the reason.
- Reversed withdrawals due to compliance review must be handled by the Compliance team, not front-line support.
- The customer's account balance must be restored before any communication is sent.

## Withdrawal Limits

- Standard daily withdrawal limit: €5,000.
- Standard monthly withdrawal limit: €20,000.
- VIP customers may have higher limits as defined by their account tier.
- Withdrawals exceeding the daily limit require manual approval from the Finance team.

## Wagering Requirements

- Withdrawals are blocked if active bonus wagering requirements have not been met.
- Support agents must check the customer's bonus status before investigating a failed withdrawal.
- If wagering requirements are the cause, explain the remaining requirements to the customer clearly.

## Escalation

- Escalate to Payments Operations if: three or more failed attempts, provider-side errors, or the customer has been waiting longer than the stated processing time.
- Escalate to Compliance if: the withdrawal was reversed due to a compliance hold or the customer's identity verification is incomplete.
