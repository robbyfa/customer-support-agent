# Account Verification Policy

## Overview

This policy governs Know Your Customer (KYC) verification requirements. All customers must complete identity verification before making their first withdrawal or when requested by the Compliance team.

## Verification Requirements

- **Proof of Identity**: valid government-issued photo ID (passport, national ID card, or driving licence).
- **Proof of Address**: utility bill, bank statement, or official government letter dated within the last 3 months.
- **Proof of Payment**: screenshot or photo of the payment method used (credit card with middle digits obscured, e-wallet account screenshot, or bank statement showing the transaction).

## Verification Process

1. Customer uploads documents through the account verification section.
2. Documents are reviewed by the Compliance team within **24–48 hours**.
3. If documents are approved, the account is marked as "verified".
4. If documents are rejected, the customer is notified with the reason and asked to resubmit.

## Common Rejection Reasons

- Document is expired.
- Image is blurry or unreadable.
- Name on the document does not match the account name.
- Proof of address is older than 3 months.
- Card number is not properly obscured (full number visible).

## Pending Verification

- If a customer's verification has been pending for more than 48 hours:
  1. Check the verification queue for the customer's submission.
  2. If documents are in the queue, inform the customer of the expected timeline.
  3. If no documents are found, ask the customer to re-upload.
  4. Escalate to the Compliance team if the review is overdue.

## Withdrawal Restrictions

- Unverified customers cannot process withdrawals.
- If a customer requests a withdrawal before completing verification, inform them of the requirement and guide them through the document upload process.
- Do not process exceptions to verification requirements without Compliance team approval.

## Escalation

- Escalate to the Compliance team if: verification is overdue (more than 48 hours), the customer disputes a document rejection, or enhanced due diligence is required.
