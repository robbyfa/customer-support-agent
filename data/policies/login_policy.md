# Login & Account Access Policy

## Overview

This policy covers customer login issues, account lockouts, and password resets. Account access issues must be handled promptly to minimize customer frustration.

## Account Lockouts

- Accounts are automatically locked after **5 consecutive failed login attempts**.
- The lockout period is **30 minutes** by default.
- If the customer contacts support during the lockout period, the agent may unlock the account manually after verifying the customer's identity.
- Identity verification for unlock requires: registered email address, date of birth, and the last four digits of the registered payment method.

## Password Reset

- Customers can request a password reset via the "Forgot Password" link on the login page.
- The reset link is sent to the registered email address and expires after **1 hour**.
- If the customer does not receive the reset email:
  1. Verify the email address on file is correct.
  2. Ask the customer to check their spam or junk folder.
  3. Resend the reset link manually from the admin panel.
  4. If the issue persists, escalate to the Technical team.

## Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)

- Customers who have enabled 2FA and lose access to their authenticator app must verify their identity before 2FA can be reset.
- Required verification: government-issued ID matching the account name and a selfie holding the ID.
- 2FA reset requests must be escalated to the Security team for review.

## Suspicious Login Activity

- If a customer reports login attempts they did not make, the account must be temporarily suspended and escalated to the Security team.
- The customer should be advised to change their password immediately upon regaining access.
- Support agents must **not** disclose IP addresses or device information to the customer.

## Escalation

- Escalate to the Technical team if: password reset emails are not being delivered after multiple attempts.
- Escalate to the Security team if: suspicious login activity is reported, 2FA reset is required, or the customer claims their account was compromised.
