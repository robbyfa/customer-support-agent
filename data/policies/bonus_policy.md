# Bonus Policy

## Overview

This policy governs how bonuses, promotions, and free bets are managed. All bonus-related inquiries must be handled according to the terms and conditions agreed upon by the customer at the time of opt-in.

## Bonus Types

- **Welcome Bonus**: offered to new customers upon first deposit. Typically a 100% match up to €100.
- **Reload Bonus**: periodic deposit match offers for existing customers.
- **Free Bet**: a bet placed at no cost to the customer, with winnings credited as bonus funds or real funds depending on the promotion.
- **Loyalty Bonus**: awarded based on customer activity and tier level.
- **Cashback Bonus**: a percentage of net losses returned to the customer over a defined period.

## Wagering Requirements

- All bonuses come with wagering requirements that must be completed before withdrawal.
- Standard wagering requirement: 30x the bonus amount.
- Wagering progress is tracked in the customer's bonus dashboard.
- Only bets placed on eligible markets count toward wagering.
- If wagering requirements are not met before the bonus expiry date, the bonus and any associated winnings are forfeited.

## Bonus Not Credited

- If a customer claims their bonus was not applied:
  1. Check whether the customer entered the correct promo code at the time of deposit.
  2. Verify that the qualifying deposit meets the minimum amount.
  3. Check if the customer already has an active bonus (only one bonus can be active at a time).
  4. If the bonus was missed due to a system error, escalate to the Promotions team for manual credit.

## Bonus Forfeiture

- Bonuses are automatically forfeited if wagering requirements are not met within the validity period.
- If a customer requests a withdrawal before completing wagering requirements, the bonus and bonus winnings may be removed from their balance.
- Forfeited bonuses cannot be reinstated unless a system error is confirmed.

## Escalation

- Escalate to the Promotions team if: the customer claims a bonus was not credited despite meeting all conditions, or if a manual bonus credit is required.
- Escalate to Compliance if: bonus abuse is suspected (multiple accounts, exploiting promotional terms).
